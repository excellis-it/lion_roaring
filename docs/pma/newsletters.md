# Newsletters

## What this is

**Newsletters** in the User PMA Admin Portal is the list of people who subscribed to the **public website newsletter** (marketing site signup).

Staff review or remove subscriber rows here. It is **not** a full email campaign builder, and it is **not** the E-Learning / E-Store newsletter tooling.

---

## Who can use it

| Need | Permission |
|------|------------|
| Open Newsletters | **Manage Newsletters** |
| Delete a subscriber row | **Delete Newsletters** |

---

## Country visibility

| Viewer | Sees |
|--------|------|
| Global | Subscribers from all countries |
| Regional / non-global | Subscribers for **their country** only |

Someone who signed up while browsing Country A’s site will not appear on Country B’s Regional staff list.

---

## Common confusion

1. **“Where do I send the newsletter?”** — This screen stores subscribers; sending may be handled outside this list depending on your ops process.  
2. **“I edited Footer newsletter title”** — Footer copy is under **Pages (CMS)** → Footer; subscribers are here.  
3. **E-Learning newsletter tools** — different product; see E-Learning docs if that is what you need.
