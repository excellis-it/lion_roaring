# Our Governance

## What this is

**Our Governance** manages public governance entries (pages under the public governance URLs). Staff maintain titles, content, order, and country-specific versions here.

---

## Who can use it

| Action | Permission |
|--------|------------|
| Manage list | **Manage Our Governance** |
| Create | **Create Our Governance** |
| Edit / Delete | Edit / Delete Our Governance |

---

## Rules that matter

1. **Content is per country** — Global editors may pick content country (often default US); Regional editors edit their country only.  
2. **Ordering** — you can reorder items within a country. Order numbers are resequenced when items are deleted or moved between countries.  
3. **US draft / prefill** — if you are viewing US-prefilled drafts for a country that has no saved row yet, drag-reorder may ignore those drafts until they are saved as real rows for that country.  
4. Deleting an entry can renumber siblings — expected, not a bug.
