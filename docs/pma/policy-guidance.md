# Policy & Guidance

## What this is

**Policy & Guidance** is the document library for policy and guidance files: upload, browse, view, download, and delete (when allowed).

### Do not confuse with website legal pages

| This library | Website legal pages |
|--------------|---------------------|
| Internal/document store under Policy & Guidance | **Pages (CMS)** → Privacy Policy, Terms, PMA Terms, Register Agreements |
| Country-scoped file list | Public HTML pages visitors read |
| Managed with Manage Policy permissions | Managed with Manage … Page permissions |

Staff often look in the wrong place when asked to “update the privacy policy.” Public privacy text is usually **Pages (CMS)**.

---

## Who can use it

| Need | Permission |
|------|------------|
| Open the menu | **Manage Policy** |
| Upload | **Upload Policy** |
| View / Download | View / Download Policy |
| Delete | **Delete Policy** |

---

## Country scoping

Same rules as **Strategy** and **Education**:

- Super Admin — broad / choosable  
- Global site — GL scope  
- Regional site — own country  
- Ecclesia filters may narrow the list further  

If a file is missing for a colleague, compare country, host, and ecclesia access before re-uploading.

---

## Typical workflow

1. Confirm whether the request is for **this library** or a **public CMS legal page**.  
2. Open Policy & Guidance.  
3. Upload or replace within the correct country scope.  
4. Tell viewers which site (Global vs regional) they must use if they are Regional/G_R.
