# Services

## What this is

**Services** is the CMS for **per-organization services** shown on the public site (`/service/{slug}` style pages).

Unlike most menus with fixed labels, the Services submenu is **built dynamically**: one link per organization currently available from the organizations list helper.

---

## Who can use it

Permission: **Manage Services**.

---

## Why the submenu looks empty

If **Our Organizations** has no organizations (for your country/visibility), the Services children can be empty even though you have Manage Services. Create or publish organizations first, then return here.

---

## Country rules

Service content follows the same content-country rules as other CMS modules (Global picker vs Regional lock; US prefill when a country has no row yet).
